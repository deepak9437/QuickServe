Who We Are

A platform built for Odisha's families

QuickServe is a home-services marketplace that connects customers with skilled local professionals — electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, cleaners, and AC repair technicians — at their doorstep.

We're not just another booking app. We're a community-first platform built to create real opportunities for local professionals while giving every household in Odisha fast, reliable help.

  • Every provider is background-verified before onboarding
  • Real-time booking with same-day slot availability
  • Transparent pricing — no hidden charges, ever
  • Rated and reviewed by real customers after each job
