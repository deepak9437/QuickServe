About QuickServe

Connecting customers with trusted local service providers quickly, securely, and efficiently.

Who We Are

QuickServe is a service marketplace platform that helps customers find skilled professionals such as electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, cleaners, and AC repair technicians.

Our goal is to simplify service booking and create opportunities for local professionals to grow their businesses.

Our Mission

To make local service booking simple, transparent, and accessible for everyone while helping service providers connect with more customers.
